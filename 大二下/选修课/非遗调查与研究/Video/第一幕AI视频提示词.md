# 第一幕 AI 视频提示词

用途：直接复制到 AI 视频生成工具中使用。  
建议：每个镜头单独生成，不要一次生成完整 10 秒。

## 全局风格提示词

每个镜头都建议带上这段，用来统一画风：

```text
cute chibi anime style, traditional Chinese shadow puppetry, warm yellow backlight, white translucent puppet screen, wooden folk theatre stage, red black gold leather shadow puppet patterns, magical but playful atmosphere, soft cinematic lighting, clean composition, high quality, smooth motion, no mouth dialogue, subtitles added later
```

## 全局负面提示词

每个镜头都建议带上这段，减少跑偏：

```text
no text, no subtitles generated in image, no logo, no watermark, no horror, no dark portal, no neon cyberpunk, no modern stage lights, no realistic adults, no extra characters, no distorted face, no broken hands, no messy background, no plastic toy look, no 3D figurine look
```

## 角色发声规则

视频中不要让角色说完整人话，字幕后期添加。

- 西格莉卡：实际只发出“西西”
- 达妮娅：实际只发出“娅娅”
- 菲比：实际只发出“菲比啾比”
- 弗诺诺：实际只发出“诺诺”
